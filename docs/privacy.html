Privacy policy

Aster collects no data. Your photos, your edits and your searches stay on your Mac.

  • Aster has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send crash reports of its own.
  • Your photos and their information (EXIF, GPS position, titles, keywords) are never sent anywhere.
  • Everything is computed on your Mac: RAW development, retouching, the automatic masks for people, skin, subject and sky, healing, search.
  • Edits and metadata are written to XMP files next to your photos. Aster also keeps an index and thumbnails on your Mac, in its own container, to stay fast; they can be rebuilt from your folders at any time.
  • To reopen the folders you chose, Aster stores sandbox bookmarks in its preferences on your Mac.
  • The only network access is the update of the Lensfun lens database, used for lens corrections. About once a week, Aster downloads the public database from lensfun.github.io (or its mirror wilson.bronger.org). The request contains no information about you or your photos. You can turn it off in Aster's Settings.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; Aster receives no payment details.
